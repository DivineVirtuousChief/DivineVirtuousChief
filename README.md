## Governance work in progress

### NJC-ARCH-001 — Validation & Assurance Architecture

A developing, evidence-centered validation concept grounded in the principle:

**VERITAS ANTE ASSERTIONEM — Truth Before Assertion.**

The proposed framework emphasizes:
- Automated validation followed by independent human review
- Separation between workload identities and human release authority
- Versioned baselines, reproducible checks, and traceable evidence
- Audit-ledger integrity and least-privilege access
- Explicit treatment of failed, skipped, and inconclusive tests

This is a proposed internal governance and technical assurance framework. It does not claim governmental, judicial, or regulatory authority, and technical test results are not a substitute for independent legal or institutional review.
